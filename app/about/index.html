Part of Towson.
Part of the family.

A familiar stop on York Road, Towson Diner has been part of local life since the 1950s — a place where generations have gathered around the table.

A family tradition.

In 1994, father and son Sam and Nick Kourtsounis bought the diner. They made it a family-run, welcoming place for neighbors, regulars and anyone stopping by for a good meal.

That same care goes into the kitchen, with quality ingredients in our cooking and baking. All of our desserts are baked right here on the premises.
