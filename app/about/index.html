If you grew up in the Baltimore area, you have probably heard of Towson Diner. A York Road staple since 1957, it has become a piece of family history for so many of our neighbors.

In 1994, father and son Sam and Nick Kourtsounis bought the diner and made it a family-run, family-friendly place where everyone is welcome.

We use only the finest ingredients in our cooking and baking. Breakfast is served all day, and every dessert is baked right here on the premises.
