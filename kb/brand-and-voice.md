# Brand and voice
Official website uses a red diner sign, cream backgrounds and dark text, with welcoming direct copy. Since 1957 and all-day breakfast are supported messages. [S1][S2]
Design interpretation: warm red/cream cues, restrained confident typography and real diner/food photos. Preserve approachable diner character; no unsupported luxury promises.
Use concise copy about meals, home baking, menu, ordering and visits. No fabricated testimonials/ratings, exact hours or current ownership claims. No decorative numbering; at most two complementary font families.
